# D10 — pivotal hosting and coordination

[P] n hosts choose H/N; at least m are needed. H costs h>0 even on failure.
Host i receives ω_i V on successful service, ω_iV≥0. Population types and their
joint law are specified; credible payments and physical capacity are separate.

[B: lab] **Grade: kernel-checked.** With J other volunteers, H changes success
only when J=m−1, so its gain is ω_i V·Pr(J=m−1)−h. The expectation algebra is
`Payoffs.hosting_pivotal_gain`/`hosting_best_response`. For every m≥2,
`all_N_strict` checks actual deterministic payoffs: neither zero nor one host
can meet m, and H loses h. This result has no n=1,m=1 extension; E4.md gives the
general pure-equilibrium characterization. Standard axioms only.

[B: lab] **Grade: computed exact.** For n=5,m=2,h=1/2,V=2 and independent types
ω∈{0,1/4,1} with probabilities {1/2,1/4,1/4}, all 7,776 action profiles are
checked from utilities. Successful equilibrium exists iff at least two types
have ω≥1/4, giving13/16. Strictly willing active pairs require two ω=1, giving
47/128. All-N persists for every type profile. `audit.py` matches the expected
number of successful equilibria as well. These are existence probabilities.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** If all types are the same common draw with
these marginals, qualifying support exists with probability1/2. The independent
binomial result therefore does not transfer to correlated populations. Five
aliases under one physical-capacity controller cannot meet two independent
resource groups. Truthful zero-weight hosts all decline; the decoder supplies no
preference or resource. These controls use their declared control/ownership map.

[B: lab] **Grade: refuted.** “Only prior reciprocal hosting or love can host a
nonreciprocating recipient” is too strong. A self-interested verifiable provider
paid3/4 at cost1/2 gains1/4. Payment can itself be contractual; the witness
refutes the stated exhaustive mechanism claim, not every role of contract.

[Q] Credible finance and a successful selection rule remain obligations unless
explicitly supplied. E4 gives a funded designated-provider construction; D19
is not obtained by composing these examples automatically.
