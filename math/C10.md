# C10 — joint feasibility and costly deferral

[P] Two retained claimants each have an elective claim for one unit. Current
capacity is1; protected baseline care is separately funded. Current admitted
allocations are(0,0),(1,0),(0,1). A reserve-funded build costs1 and makes next-date
capacity2. Both claimants must authorize a one-date deadline extension, with
their claims and identities unchanged. Their waiting permission is an explicit
E4 purchase, not inferred from collective infeasibility.

[B: lab] **Grade: computed exact.** No current allocation supplies both claims.
The two-period renegotiated plan spends reserve1, carries both claims forward,
then allocates(1,1). It passes exactly when reserve≥1 and both waiting permissions
are present. Reserve0 and one refused deadline extension are negative controls.
`python3 covenant_lab/carriers.py` enumerates the immediate assignments and
checks those admission predicates.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** For this divisible single-resource carrier,
current feasibility is Σneeds≤capacity. Authorized deferral repairs feasibility
only if the financed later capacity meets all retained needs before the newly
accepted deadlines. It cannot count as success under the original unextended
deadline. This is a target-preserving change of an authorized temporal contract,
not deletion of a claimant or a theorem that all incompatible preferences can
be reconciled. Requests lacking funding or consent honestly return infeasible.
