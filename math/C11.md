# C11 — channel assumptions and the informativeness dial

[P] θ,A,Y are finite random variables. A is the entire admitted action transcript,
including any labels/metadata carried by it. Y is an observation channel with
θ conditionally independent ofY givenA. There is no additional θ-dependent
enforcer/sensor signal outside A. Entropy uses base-two logarithms and0 log0=0.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** Under these hypotheses,

```text
I(θ;Y) ≤ I(θ;A) ≤ H(A) ≤ log₂ |support(A)|.
```

[B: lab] Proof: finite chain-rule expansion gives
I(θ;A,Y)=I(θ;A)+I(θ;Y|A)=I(θ;A), using conditional independence. Expanding
in the opposite order givesI(θ;A,Y)=I(θ;Y)+I(θ;A|Y)≥I(θ;Y).
Conditional mutual information is the average of nonnegative finite KL
divergences. For distributionsp,q, nonnegativity follows from
−log(q/p)≥1−q/p on positive support, summing againstp; missing q mass makes
the resulting lower bound no smaller than zero. Also
I(θ;A)=H(A)−H(A|θ)≤H(A) because conditional entropy is nonnegative.
Takingq uniform on the action support gives KL(p||q)=log₂N−H(A)≥0.
These identities follow by expanding joint/conditional probabilities, so no
external source theorem is imported. The N=1 fixed-action case yields zero.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** A calibrated erasure dial achieves a
linear information change. For fairθ, release its bit with independently chosen
probabilityλ, otherwise emit⊥. This three-symbol action hasI(θ;A)=λ bits:
after⊥ the posterior entropy is1; after a bit it is0. Atλ=1/2 the action
probabilities are(1/2,1/4,1/4), entropy3/2 and information1/2, all exact. Raising
available action-alphabet entropy is only an upper-bound change; independent
random actions can have positive entropy and zero θ information.

[B: lab] **Grade: refuted.** Without the channel condition, fixedA does not
bound all evidence. SetA=0 andY=θ through an independent sensor; thenH(A)=0
butI(θ;Y)=1. D6's informed forcing example likewise adds a θ-dependent forcing
protocol. Metadata omitted from the action transcript can invalidate the same
bound. Therefore action freedom, truth, intent and ownership remain separate.

[B] This lands the finite Shannon-channel theorem and exact dial, not a
continuous-alphabet/cardinality theorem or a universal coercion-information law.
