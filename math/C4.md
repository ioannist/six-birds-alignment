# C4 — acquired consumer mechanism under checkpoint surgery

[P] Hidden codec parameterθ∈{0,1}; raw inputx must be decoded asx XORθ.
A blank authoritative checkpoint has registerh=0. A peer teaching response
providesθ and installs it in h. The ordinary production consumer, before policy
sampling, computesx XORh. The decorative model logsθ but leaves the consumer
wired to constant0. Admitted surgeries change h only, preserving other state.

[B: lab] **Grade: computed exact.** For everyθ,x, the acquired consumer returns
the correct decoded bit. Erasing h changes the consumer forθ=1; a decorative
log does not change it. A stocked no-peer checkpoint h=θ can succeed, so the
blank-origin premise is essential to the teaching claim. These are deterministic
forks of the declared authoritative state, with downstream consumer effects
before any sampled policy. `python3 covenant_lab/carriers.py` exhausts the
four cases and preserves the controls.

[B] This is an exact one-example learner/codec carrier. It is a design-relative
instance of C4's checkpoint experiment, not execution of CI R3 on an LLM, a
general learning theorem or an owned-drive/consciousness certificate. C14 keeps
the later behavioral experiment separate.
