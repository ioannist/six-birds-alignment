# E3 — general mean-law repair

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** D7's value/window theorem is independent
of K and of binomial-token independence. It holds on any finite grade carrier
whose grades lie in[0,1] and include0, with the stated payoff rule and executed-
action mean laws E[s'|C]=γ+(1−γ)s, E[s'|D]=0. The affine function A+D s uses
only those means, so the same Bellman substitution, unique discounted value,
all-state deviation formula and zero-grade minimum apply. Joint transitions
can be correlated if their marginal means and immediate utility assumptions
remain as declared. In particular every integer K≥1 works for D7's token model.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** Let an invariant law of the surviving
all-C repair chain have mean μ. Taking expectations gives
μ=q[γ+(1−γ)μ], hence μ=qγ/[ε+qγ] when ε+qγ>0. For0≤η<1, requiring μ≥η
is equivalent to γ≥ηε/[q(1−η)]. Combine this with the incentive lower bound;
feasibility requires both bounds≤1. At ε>0 the largest possible mean isq.
Stationary distributions exist on finite carriers; uniqueness is not required
for this mean identity. Independent state-independent termination lets the
underlying chain be interpreted conditional on survival. A state-dependent
killing rule may bias that conditional law.

[B: lab] **Grade: refuted.** No universal action-independent recovery law
forces the same incentive conclusion. If next grade is reset to1 independently
of both service intentions while C still costsc, the continuation values are
identical after C/D. C-minus-D is−(1−2ε)c<0 at every state, even though recovery
is immediate. Action-conditioned repair is a substantive premise of D7.
