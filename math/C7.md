is0 irrespective ofθ. Waiting advances the clock; fromt=1 onward the chosen

observes it through its consumer. No altered historical debt is declared.

current readout and different one-step future preference outputs0,1. The washout
control makes both one-step outputs0. `python3 covenant_lab/carriers.py` checks
these exact readouts. The distinction is causally consumed after the delay;
an unused hidden bit would have failed the future split.
