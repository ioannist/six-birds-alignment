# D9 — funded care and capacity

[P] Two productive actors have (b,c)=(3,1),(4,3/2), renewable endowments4,3,
own-care needs1,1 and escrow taxes1/2 each. A third actor has singleton service
action D, endowment0 and care need1. Inability has no standing debit. Utility
care/tax increments are independent of service action and public counter state.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** For any stochastic discounted service
kernel and constant increment κ_i, W_i'=W_i+κ_i/(1−δ) satisfies the shifted
Bellman equation. Every deviation is shifted by κ_i+δκ_i/(1−δ)=κ_i/(1−δ)
as well. Hence every slack is unchanged. This is conditional on the increment
being constant; entry, tax refusal or withdrawal changes are additional choices.

[B: lab] **Grade: computed exact.** Independent matrices reproduce every
heterogeneous slack at δ=3/4 and9/10 and the constant shift. Productive budgets
are5/2,3/2. Changing the first endowment to2 leaves budget1/2<c_0; halving
both taxes underfunds third-person care. Positive slacks do not imply physical
feasibility or floor solvency. Command: `python3 covenant_lab/audit.py`.

[B: lab] **Grade: refuted.** A fresh voluntary tax with no private consequences
is not incentive-compatible: withholding t>0 saves exactly t. Thus the fixed
escrow result does not establish endogenous financing. Nor does a singleton
patient action supply political voice, privacy, appeal or fission governance.
