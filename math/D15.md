## Atomic-service variant with a coalition cap

[P] This is a **new mechanism**, not a proof that the original donation/reset
policy is coalition-proof. Two actors choose C,D or safe exit before fresh
independent implementation errors. Executed offer probabilities are q_i=1−ε_i,
ε_i and0 respectively, where0≤ε_i≤1/2. Elective service executes atomically only
if both actual offers are1. Actor i then gets net matched value B_i=b_i−c_i≥0;
its own executed offer incurs preparation a_i even if matching fails.
Assume0≤a_i≤B_i q_j. Realized matching, calibration, access, independently
funded action-independent care/records/debt/appeal baselines and risk-neutral
discounted utility are declared premises. Exit incurs no elective preparation
and preserves the baseline. No future execution error is visible before choice.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** For every intended joint profile and
either actor i, its conditional expected stage payoff is

    r_i = p_i(B_i p_j−a_i) ≤ q_i(B_i q_j−a_i) = M_i.

Proof:0≤p_i≤q_i,0≤p_j≤q_j and B_i≥0 first give
p_i(B_i p_j−a_i)≤p_i(B_i q_j−a_i). The coefficient B_i q_j−a_i≥0 then gives
the second inequality. CC attains M_i simultaneously for both actors.
Jointly correlated mixed offers are handled by conditioning on the intended
joint profile and averaging this inequality; factoring mixed marginal offer
probabilities would be invalid. Past-dependent coordination cannot alter the
fresh-error conditional bound.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** For every0≤δ<1, always-CC after every
history is strong Nash against arbitrary bounded history-dependent/randomized
binding coalition plans, with balanced internal transfers. For any finite
coalition S, summing the conditional bound, taking expectations and summing
the convergent discounted series gives

    Σ_{i∈S} U_i ≤ (Σ_{i∈S} M_i)/(1−δ).

Internal transfers cancel in this sum at each outcome. Always-CC attains the
bound for every member, so no coalition can weakly improve all members and
strictly improve one. Adding the fixed care/record baseline cancels on both
sides. Individual best-response optimality is the singleton case, at every
history. Since **all** binding improvements are excluded, internally stable
coalition improvements are excluded as well on this alphabet. Every always-CC
continuation is componentwise maximal, giving a Pareto renegotiation criterion
without relying on promised punishments. This is a scoped coalition-proof
variant, not a general institution/physical implementation theorem.
