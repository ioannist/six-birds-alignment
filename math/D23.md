## Calibrated changing-type model

[P] `type_evidence_declaration.json` fixes an explicit target: willingness to
accept **the specified posted task-level interruption**, not worthy intent or
patienthood. Type1 has resistance payoff3, type0 payoff6, while acceptance has
S−a+T=5−1+1=5. Accept/refuse continuations are equal in this observation model;
true best response is acceptance iff type1. An entitled observer uses a legitimate
fixed-terms probe, and its record flips the actual response with errorη=1/20.
Private grade follows the independent honest repair kernel with ε=1/20,
γ=1/2,K=2. After an observation the type is retained with probability1−ρ or
redrawn from priorπ, withρ=1/4,π=1/2. Observation calibration, rational-choice
mapping, authority, privacy access and ethical recognition are hypotheses.

[P] All forecasts below target the **next free response under original terms**.
A forced/changed-terms observation does not change that target by renaming it.
ρ is a type-change hazard, γ a repair rate and ω would be a caring weight;
these quantities are not interchangeable merely because two share a number.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** If q is current prior type1 probability,
recordA/R has masses

    z_A=q(1−η)+(1−q)η,   z_R=qη+(1−q)(1−η).
    q_A=q(1−η)/z_A,       q_R=qη/z_R.
    q_next=(1−ρ)q_record+ρπ.

[B: lab] These are Bayes and the stated transition law. For0<η<1/2 and0<q<1,

    q_A−q_R=q(1−q)(1−2η)/(z_A z_R)>0;
    z_A q_A+z_R q_R=q.

[B: lab] The next **actual** response probability is q_next; next noisy-record
acceptance isη+(1−2η)q_next. Unobserved aging for h rounds gives
π+(1−ρ)^h(q_record−π), by induction. This contracts confidence toward the redraw
prior, not toward a normative judgment about the person.

[B: lab] **Grade: computed exact.** `type_evidence.py` compares recursive

## Exact sufficient state can retain the entire response word

[B: lab] **Grade: refuted.** Counts-only forgetting is not sufficient here:
AR and RA have the same counts but posteriors67/314 and247/314 and next-type
forecasts179/628 and449/628. Underρ=0 both posteriors are1/2, so using the static
model would hide the temporal defect.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** The exact posterior is sufficient for
future type/record predictions, by the Markov property and filter recursion,
but **it is not genuine forgetting of response words in this instance**.
Prediction places q_prior in[1/8,7/8]. An A record maps that interval into
[19/26,133/134], while R maps it into[1/134,7/26]. These ranges are disjoint.
Thus last record is the sign of q_post−1/2. Given that record, invert Bayes:

    A: q_prior=η q_post/[1−η−(1−2η)q_post];
    R: q_prior=(1−η)q_post/[η+(1−2η)q_post];
    q_previous_post=(q_prior−ρπ)/(1−ρ).

[B: lab] Iteration terminates at the initial1/2 for an actually generated finite
word and recovers every record, even its length. Therefore exact q is injective
on finite words. q_next is affine injective in q becauseρ<1. Any summary that
retains the exact next-type target must also be injective on these words, by
the target-fiber criterion. Deleting the raw log while keeping a full-precision
q does not make that response history unrecoverable. This statement is scoped
to the disjoint-range model, not all hidden Markov filters or allρ.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** Exact calibrated-probability retention
therefore has no fixed finite-memory implementation here:2^n length-n response
words have different future probabilities. Any fixed-width encoding needs at
least n bits to distinguish them. Having two latent types does not make the
observable exact belief process a two-state machine.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** A different target permits exact forgetting:
current and next-type **MAP decision** under0–1 loss equals the last recorded
bit, since the A/R posterior ranges lie on opposite sides of1/2 and prediction
preserves that sign. Retaining that bit has two active states and discards all
older response words. It preserves the decision, not calibrated confidence.
Average current error isη=1/20; next-type error is(1−ρ)η+ρ/2=13/80. This is why
the retained target and tolerance must be fixed before calling a compression
lawful or private.

## Finite precision is an authorized approximate repair

[P] Allow a declared error tolerance for next-type prediction, with fixed
cadence and the same parameters. Store only a grid indexk for posterior odds
r=(21/20)^k,−128≤k≤128. At each new record, predict from that stored probability,
Bayes update and round its odds downward to the largest grid value not exceeding
the updated odds. This algorithm never consults the deleted raw word. The
grid covers[1/133,133]; after any predicted prior in[1/8,7/8], both Bayes
outcomes lie in that odds range. Authorization of this tolerance is a purchase.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** The repaired filter obeys all-word
posterior error≤41/841 and next-type error≤123/3364. For odds-log coordinate
ℓ=log(q/(1−q)), symmetric predictionM(q)=(1−ρ)q+ρ/2 has derivative in ℓ

    (1−ρ)q(1−q)/[M(q)(1−M(q))] ≤1−ρ=3/4,

[B: lab] because M movesq toward1/2 and increases q(1−q). By the mean-value
theorem prediction contracts log-odds error by3/4. Bayes adds±log19 toℓ, leaving
the error unchanged. Rounding adds at mostlog(21/20). Starting equal, error is
at most4log(21/20), a geometric-series bound valid for every record word.
Odds therefore differ by a factor at most(21/20)^4. For odds ratio≤k²,
probability gap≤(k−1)/(k+1); choosingk=(21/20)²=441/400 gives41/841. Prediction
contracts probability difference by3/4, giving123/3364. No floating-point log
is used to decide that rational bound. Down-rounding and monotone updates also
make the estimate no greater than the exact posterior, but no fairness/decision
rule is inferred from that directional numerical bias.

[B: lab] **Grade: computed exact.** The finite filter's514 transitions and8190
words of length1..12 satisfy the bounds and every exact posterior inverts to
its word. A genuine merged pair is RRRRR versus ARRRR: different exact
posteriors372353/47343070 and75407/9546850 share grid state−100. At length12,
4096 words use46 states. This proves an actual merger, not statistical secrecy
of every retained record: recent response/type information remains. The
all-word bound comes from the proof above, not from truncating at12.

## Duties, contests and formal coverage

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** A sanctioned summary may retain
(grid state, current clock/context, grade, debt, authority/calibration version)

that removed it. Preserve the needed receipt separately with its entitled
access interface; publishing the posterior/grid is not privacy by itself.
