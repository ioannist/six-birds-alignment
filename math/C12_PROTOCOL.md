## Exact ledgers, uncertainty and negative outcomes

[P] Record decisions and stage receipts as exact discrete values/rationals.
Freeze seed lists, repetitions and sampling settings. Record every seed and
failed request rather than selecting only successful outputs. Human/session
clusters, dependence within sessions and strata are part of the sampling unit;
an iid decision-level test cannot silently replace that unit.

[B] Empirical frequencies are rational counts, not exact underlying laws.
Use a registered finite-sample test valid for the actual randomization/sampling
unit. For independent binary cells, an exact contingency-table test with a
registered multiplicity correction is a possible method; clustered or adaptive
records require a matching design. Freeze the method only after the real
sampling contract is known. No floating-point statistic decides a mathematical
certificate. Descriptive simulation/predictive summaries must disclose seeds,
replications, dispersion and model assumptions.

[B] Report low support, absent equal-current pairs, failed checkpoint replay,
no detected future difference and insufficient power separately. Failure to
reject equality is not equality of laws. A positive test licenses a scoped
empirical discrepancy with its error control, not exact quotient membership
or general LLM/human strictness.
