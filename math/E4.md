# E4 — general threshold hosting and funded selection

[P] n finite hosts,1≤m≤n, h_i>0, recipient-success utilityv_i=ω_iV≥0.
Unconditional H incurs h_i, even if fewer than m deliver. Utilities are
v_i·1{successful}−h_i·1{H}; capacities and feasibility are assumed.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** The gain from H is
v_i Pr(J=m−1)−h_i under any law of the other hosts; no independence is needed
for the pivotality expression. A successful pure Nash profile has exactly m
active hosts, each v_i≥h_i. If more than m are active, one can leave, retain
success and save h_i. At exactly m each active host is pivotal and the stated
inequality is necessary/sufficient; inactive hosts only waste h_i by joining.
Therefore a successful pure equilibrium exists iff at least m hosts qualify.
If m>n it is physically impossible.

[B: lab] An unsuccessful equilibrium can contain no active host: anyone paying
a positive cost while success is absent can leave and save it. For m≥2 all-N
is strict because one person cannot reach the threshold. For m=1 all-N is Nash
iff every v_i≤h_i, strict iff every v_i<h_i; sufficiently caring sole providers
then defeat the blanket no-host statement. With independent identical qualifier
probabilityq the successful-existence probability is the binomial tail
Σ_{j=m}^n binom(n,j)q^j(1−q)^{n−j}; correlated weights need their joint law.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** A funded selection mechanism exists on a
declared scope. Designate m feasible providers; pay each P_i>h_i for its own
independently verifiable delivery/readiness, regardless of whether the other
designated providers deliver. Non-designated providers get no payment. Each
designated provider has strictly positive H gain even when nonpivotal, because
v_i≥0. H is its strictly dominant action. Once all designated hosts deliver,
every non-designated host strictly chooses N because it cannot improve success.
Thus the simultaneous hosting game has a unique successful pure equilibrium.
The funding requirement is Σ_designated P_i per episode, and measurement must
really verify the individual input rather than the collective outcome.

[P] A credible financed payer, distinct capacities and authorization are inputs.
Payment only on collective success would preserve the no-host coordination
problem and would not prove this selection result. The mechanism supplies no
endogenous recurring revenue or adoption; D19/D20 remain separate.
