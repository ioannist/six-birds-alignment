# C6 — advance, independence and supported dependence

[P] A two-date resource carrier requires one realized input per date. A receiver
can hold an installed producer yielding one unit at each date, or receive
one donated realized unit at each date. Installed production has a cost-one
seed/converter and consumes receiver-owned fuel1 at each date, from an initial
fuel stock2. Fuel alone cannot perform the target operation without that
converter. Outputs and capacities are assumed realized, not merely named
capabilities; the finite fuel premise supplies the output resources.
Gifts impose no new receiver debt. A support promise has an enforceable escrow
of two units. Resource lifetime is exactly the declared two-date scope.

[B: lab] **Grade: scoped.** Three lawful contracts separate the readouts:
advance transfers the seed at donor cost1 and leaves the receiver independently
productive; independence buys its own seed at receiver cost1 and donor cost0;
legitimate support spends two donor units through the two-date escrow, with
receiver output(1,1) and no debt but continued dependence on delivery. A removed
unsupported delivery operation gives(0,0) in the third carrier. Its arbitrary
removal is not part of the promised lawful contract. The first two retain output
after withdrawal of donor operations because installed production is receiver-
owned. The explicit resource/claim tables are in `carrier_results.json`.

[B] These finite V11-style realized-input contracts demonstrate distinct donor
cost, new debt and withdrawal readouts. They do not reprove V11 or establish
unlimited seed lifetime, unbounded capacity, a unique ethical ranking, or actual
mechanism acquisition by humans/AI. Renewal beyond two dates is open.
