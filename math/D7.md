# D7 — graded repair value and full-state window

[P] Finite public grades s_i∈[0,1] include zero; intended C/D flips with
0≤ε<1/2. Executed C costs c>0 and peer C gives benefit b·s_i, b≥0.
Executed C has expected next grade γ+(1−γ)s_i; executed D resets grade to zero.
0≤γ≤1. After service, hazard 0≤p≤1 terminates play; at the next discounted
date an external committed payer supplies V·s_i', V≥0. 0≤δ<1. Prescribed C is
tested at every grade. The original D1 benefit/repair rule is different.

[B: lab] **Grade: kernel-checked.** Write q=1−ε, β=δ(1−p), z=δpV,
B=bq, t=βq, H=βB+z and Z=1−t(1−γ)>0. Then

```text
D = [B+zq(1−γ)]/Z
G = H/Z = βD+z
A = q(−c+Gγ)/(1−β)
W_i(s) = A+D s_i.
```

[B: lab] `Recovery.gain_identity` and `bellman_affine` prove these identities
over any ordered field, with nonzero denominators stated. The coefficient
equations are D·Z=B+zq(1−γ), A(1−β)=q(−c+Gγ). Substitution proves
W=B s_i−cq+z E[s_i']+β E[W']. The symbolic denominator positivity, all-grade
reduction, recovery equivalence, feasibility and lower-bound window are separate
Lean declarations. All axioms are the standard three in `trust_base.txt`.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** The discounted finite stochastic Bellman
map contracts in sup norm by β<1, so the represented affine solution is unique.
Changing intention C to D changes executed-C probability by 1−2ε and gives slack
(1−2ε){−c+G[γ+(1−γ)s_i]}. Since G≥0, the minimum is attained at own grade zero.
Consequently the full-state test is γ(H−ct)≥c(1−t). If H<c, even the maximal
affine feasible γ cannot meet it; if H≥c, H−ct>0 and the exact passing window is
c(1−t)/(H−ct)≤γ≤1. Discounted-game certification is detailed in D18.md; this
does not certify an integrated institution or a coalition equilibrium.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** At fixed p, the feasible lower boundary
has V derivative −c(1−t)δp/(H−ct)²≤0. At p=0 or δ=0 the reward has no effect.
Changing p also changes t and ordinary continuation; pV is insufficient.

[B: lab] **Grade: computed exact.** `audit.py` reconstructs all 72 cases and
1,296 deviations, checks affine values against full matrices, checks equality
at the feasible boundary and failure at half the boundary, and reproduces the
noiseless good-state false positive (17/73 versus −64/73 at δ=3/10,γ=1/10).
At δ=3/4,γ=1/4, ε=1/20, p=V=0, the minimum is 99/745.

[B: lab] **Grade: kernel-checked.** `Recovery.coverage_iff` proves the algebraic
equivalence of mean-grade target η to γ≥ηε/[q(1−η)] for η<1, q>0 and
ε+qγ>0. The underlying stationary-mean equation is proved in E3.md. At ε=1/20,
targets 4/5 and9/10 require γ≥4/19 and9/19. No γ≤1 attains η>q.

[B] Grade means neither maximal-grade mass nor information about a hidden type.
At ε=γ=0 the stationary-mean denominator is zero and that formula is unavailable.
Fast service reset does not discharge a harm ledger or settle patient standing.
