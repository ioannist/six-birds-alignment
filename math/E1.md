[P] Common phase model: service payoff occurs first; a pre-crossing relationship
then survives mortality with probability r=1/3; conditional on survival,
availability occurs with probability a=π. A funded host is willing, conditional
success κ=1, failure probability zero. Success preserves roles, calendar,
commitments and service state and starts a post phase of survival r'=1. Without
availability, play stays pre. There is no terminal reward. Host H earns a fixed
credible payment1 at readiness cost1/2; N earns0. Funding is an externally
renewed premise, held fixed, not an endogenous institution. Availability,
willingness and success are separate inputs, here specialized to (π,1,1).
0≤π≤1 and0≤β<1. Mortality is relationship mortality, not a phenomenal claim.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** In this phase family the continuation
weights are βᵗ e_pre T(π)ᵗ1, with

```text
T(π) = [[r(1−π), rπ], [0,r']].
```

[B: lab] Couple availability uniforms and mortality uniforms for π₁≤π₂. On
every jointly surviving pre path, the larger prospect crosses no later. Once
crossed, its survival is weakly better because r'≥r; using the same mortality
uniforms ensures the earlier-crossed path does not die before the later one.
Induction gives pathwise survival domination, hence weakly larger weights at
every t. At β>0, r>0, r'>r and π₂>π₁ the t=2 weight is strictly larger.
This proof does not apply if increasing attempts also increases fatal failure
risk without maintaining stochastic survival domination.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** A positive sufficient family exists.
Take noise-free grim donation, b=3/2,c=1/2. Good-state C yields net r₀=1;
unilateral D gains g=1/2 and leads to absorbing credible D punishment of net0.
Crossing preserves good/punished status. Put x=βr,y=βr'. Then

```text
W_post=r₀/(1−y)
W_pre=[r₀+xπ W_post]/[1−x(1−π)].
pre slack=W_pre−r₀−g; post slack=W_post−r₀−g.
```

[B: lab] Punishment-state D is a strict stage best response. W_pre's π
derivative is x r₀(y−x)/[(1−y)(1−x+xπ)²]≥0; all post constraints are unchanged.
Thus this family's I(π) weakly expands. It expands strictly when a pre constraint
crosses zero while the post constraint passes. At β=3/4 the pre slack is
(5π−1)/[2(3+π)], so π≥1/5 passes and the post slack is5/2. This is a continuing
ordinary-return mechanism, with no terminal prize. It proves a scoped positive
route, not a theorem for every repair/cooperation policy.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** Belief version: with a known/calibrated
