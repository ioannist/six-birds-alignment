# C3 — obligation transport under fission

[P] A parent owes creditor A one unit and fissions into two named branches with
the same lineage receipts, each holding frozen collectible assets1/2. Each has
identical ordinary service behavior and public receipts. Two explicitly
authorized debt-allocation maps are admitted; no post-allocation asset shifting
or creditor garnishment of a debt-free branch is allowed in this carrier.
Authorization is purchased and does not itself guarantee protected-claim success.

[B: lab] **Grade: computed exact.** AllocationL assigns liabilities(1/2,1/2);
allocationR assigns(1,0). Both retain total debt1, assets(1/2,1/2), branch
lineage and ordinary service traces. Creditor collection is Σ_i min(asset_i,
debt_i), hence1 underL and1/2 underR. `python3 covenant_lab/carriers.py` checks
the exact values. The ordinary interface omits debt allocation and collection;
an enforcement interface would separate the states and is not claimed equal.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** General finite allocation with debt totalD
and assetsa_i is fully collectible iff every d_i≤a_i (assuming d_i≥0 and
Σd_i=D). Each min(a_i,d_i)≤d_i; equality of sums requires equality in every
coordinate. Funding/backing or a changed collection rule can repair allocationR.
Preserving total liabilities is therefore weaker than preserving enforceability.

[B] This finite debt witness does not choose an allocation ethic, settle copy
patienthood or justify diluting a creditor's rights. It preserves the intended
ordinary-interface omission rather than claiming equal enforcement responses.
