## Public contract, sensor and donor-perspective thresholds

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** The public-contract and common-sensor
candidate is Markov-perfect iff

    δ(1−2ν)x ≥ c,

where ν is action-report error, η=ε+(1−2ε)ν, q=1−2ε, and

    A = 1−2δ/3,
    D = A+δη/6,
    Δ = A D−δ²η²/18
      = [1−δ(2−η)/3][1−δ(4+η)/6],
    x = q[b D/3+δη c/18]/Δ,
    y = −q[c A/6+δη b/18]/Δ,
    a = [ε(b−c)/3+δ(x+2y)/3]/(1−δ),
    W_i(s)=a+x g_i+y k_i.

the two computed sensor cases have ν=0 and29/4000. Here g_i is own public
eligibility and k_i counts the two other eligible actors. The seven reachable
vectors exclude all-bad; for a fixed actor they form **five**, not six, (g,k)
classes. Values can also be extended to an artificially initialized all-bad
vector, but that vector is not required to enlarge the declared carrier.
