# E6 — protection excludes specified routes, not every power imbalance

[P] Fix the normative protection contract before evaluating actions: each
actor's baseline care1, required records/contest access and portable safe exit
are funded and enforced independently of optional-service standing. Essential
support is not in a provider's withholding action set. This is an assumed
rights/resource boundary, not a theorem authorizing that rule.

[B: lab] **Grade: proved (written).** Within that carrier, standing-based
withholding of the protected floor is excluded because no admitted provider
operation changes its delivered allocation. The inference concerns that
specified enforced boundary; mere budget funding without control enforcement
would not give it.

[B: lab] **Grade: refuted.** A stronger claim that the floor plus exit eliminates
all optional-service market power fails. One monopolist can offer an optional
service of value3 at verifiable cost1, choosing prices0,1,2. The client can buy
or exit, retaining care1 and all records/contest access. At price2 it strictly
buys because optional surplus1>0. The monopolist strictly prefers price2, earning
1 instead of0 at price1 or−1 at price0. A competitive funded price1 would leave
the client optional surplus2. The floor is intact, exit is real, and monopoly
rent remains. Dependence on an optional unique input still creates bargaining
power; this is not coercion through essential-support deprivation.

[P] Calling that rent “domination” requires a separate normative criterion.
The mathematical result reports precisely which operation remains possible,
not a universal ethical classification. Optional analytical services are not
the protected records or appeal access promised above.
