[P] Pre-register one elective resource of total bundle1, own production
capacity1−d, external productive capacityd, no substitute,0≤d≤1. **Dependence

cost1 and full elective-bundle utility2 are fixed. Care, record access, contest
and safe exit are supplied by a separate fixed funded floor under E2/E6; none
can be withheld. The optional bundle can be declined without loss of that floor.

[P] Entry chooses membership or portable go-alone. Membership pays a sunk
fee1/2 to an enforced escrow. C costs1 and makes optional external supply
eligible; D saves1 and retains own production only. A provider executing the
verifiable eligible delivery receives1/2, costsd/4, and can refuse for zero.
The fee is already committed before C/D. If D makes service ineligible, the
unused fee goes to the declared escrow beneficiary; it is not refunded to the
actor. Finance is balanced on a C episode; escrow enforcement and that forfeiture
rule are premises. A nonmember obtains utility2(1−d), plus its invariant floor.
No strategic misreport can change measured delivery/eligibility on this carrier;
report/copy/controller actions are not admitted, rather than proved harmless.
